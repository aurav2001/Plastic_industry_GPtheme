ZERO-DEFECT BENCHMARKS

World-Class Quality &
Testing Laboratory

Every batch undergoes rigorous chemical, mechanical, and environmental endurance tests to ensure 100% UN packaging compliance and aerospace-grade durability.

01

Drop Impact Test

Filled to nominal capacity with chilled anti-freeze liquid down to -18°C and dropped from heights up to 1.8 meters onto concrete without any rupture or leakage.

UN Standard 6.1.5.3
02

Hydrostatic Pressure Test

Internal hydraulic pressurization up to 250 kPa sustained for 30 minutes to verify seam integrity, gasket resilience, and zero fluid seepage.

Zero Seepage Rating
03

Stacking & Load Endurance

Subjected to top-load compressive force equivalent to a 3-meter warehouse stack at 40°C for 28 consecutive days without deformation.

1+3 Stack Endurance
04

Aero Composite Analysis

Ultrasonic non-destructive testing (NDT), vibration harmonics, and thermal resistance validation for drone airframes and avionics connectors.

Aerospace Standards
