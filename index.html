QUALITY ASSURANCE & TESTING LAB

In-House Testing According to ASTM & ISO Norms

Every production batch is tested across key mechanical, thermal, and rheological parameters before dispatch. A Certificate of Analysis (COA) is issued for each lot.

ASTM D1238 / ISO 1133
MFI

Melt Flow Index (MFI)

Precision computerized MFI tester measuring exact melt mass-flow rate to guarantee consistent flow in dies.

Equipment: Digital Extrusion Plastometer
ASTM D638 / ISO 527
UTM

Tensile & Flexural Strength

Universal Testing Machine (UTM) measuring tensile yield, elongation at break, and 3-point bending modulus.

Equipment: Microprocessor UTM (50 kN)
ASTM D256 / ISO 180
IZOD

Izod & Charpy Impact Test

Pendulum impact testing on notched test specimens to measure fracture toughness and cold resistance.

Equipment: Motorized Izod Impact Tester
CIE L*a*b* / ASTM E1347
COLOR

Color Matching (Delta E < 0.5)

Dual-beam spectrophotometer guaranteeing identical color hue across repeating production batches for OEM parts.

Equipment: X-Rite Color Spectrophotometer
