Automotive Grade

PPCP Grey Granules

MFI: 10 – 12 • Automotive & Battery Cases

SRS Polymer PPCP Grey Granules are engineered from high-grade virgin and precision reprocessed polypropylene block copolymer. Compounded with specialized thermal and processing stabilizers to ensure excellent melt flow, minimal warpage, and high impact resistance under extreme vibration and temperature cycles.

Melt Flow Index 10 - 12 g/10min
Polymer Base Block Copolymer
Standard Pack 25 Kg Bags
Pellet Form Uniform Cut

Verified Technical Specifications (ASTM Standards)

Melt Flow Index (MFI) 10 – 12 g/10min (ASTM D1238, 230°C / 2.16 kg)
Density / Specific Gravity 0.905 g/cm³ (ASTM D792)
Tensile Strength at Yield 27 MPa (ASTM D638, 50 mm/min)
Izod Impact Strength (Notched) 105 J/m (ASTM D256, 23°C)
Flexural Modulus 1,250 MPa (ASTM D790)
Elongation at Break 55% (ASTM D638)
Available Colors / Form Consistent Industrial Grey, Dark Grey (Zero Swirl)
Standard Packaging 25 Kg Net Multiwall Moisture-Barrier Laminated Bags / 1,000 Kg Jumbo
Recommended Processing High-Speed Injection Moulding (Melt: 210°C - 235°C, Mould: 30°C - 50°C)

Key Industrial & Manufacturing Applications

Automotive Battery Casings Two-Wheeler Battery Trays & Fairings Heavy-Duty Industrial Crates Electrical Terminal Boxes Agricultural Pump Covers

Recommended Moulding Guidelines

Barrel Temperature Rear: 195°C – 205°C | Middle: 210°C – 225°C | Front: 220°C – 235°C
Mould Temperature 30°C – 50°C (Water cooled for rapid cycle time)
Injection Pressure 70 – 110 MPa (Depending on wall thickness & gate design)
Pre-Drying Conditions Generally not required; if exposed to high humidity, dry at 75°C for 1.5 hours.
