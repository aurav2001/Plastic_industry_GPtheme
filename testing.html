RIGOROUS BATCH VERIFICATION

6 Key Testing Parameters & Instruments

Each polymer shipment is accompanied by a certified Batch Certificate of Analysis (COA) guaranteeing zero variation in production runs.

ASTM D1238 / ISO 1133
MFI

Melt Flow Index (MFI)

Precision computerized MFI tester measuring exact melt mass-flow rate to guarantee consistent flow in injection and extrusion dies.

Equipment: Digital Extrusion Plastometer
ASTM D638 / ISO 527
UTM

Tensile & Flexural Strength

Universal Testing Machine (UTM) measuring tensile yield, elongation at break, and 3-point flexural bending modulus.

Equipment: Microprocessor UTM (50 kN)
ASTM D256 / ISO 180
IZOD

Izod & Charpy Impact Test

Pendulum impact testing on notched test specimens to measure fracture toughness and cold resistance down to -20°C.

Equipment: Motorized Izod Impact Tester
ASTM D5630 / ISO 3451
ASH

Ash & Mineral Filler %

High-temperature muffle furnace up to 1000°C verifying precise glass fiber, talc, and calcium carbonate percentage.

Equipment: Programmed Muffle Furnace
ASTM D792 / ISO 1183
DENS

Specific Gravity & Density

Electronic immersion balance checking material purity, buoyancy, and displacement density with 4 decimal accuracy.

Equipment: Digital Densimeter (0.0001 g/cm³)
CIE L*a*b* / ASTM E1347
COLOR

Color Matching (Delta E < 0.5)

Dual-beam spectrophotometer guaranteeing identical color hue across repeating production batches for OEM parts.

Equipment: X-Rite Color Spectrophotometer

Our Standard 4-Step Quality Control Protocol

Step 01

Raw Material Screening

Spectroscopy & moisture analysis on all virgin resin and pre-sorted post-industrial feedstocks before compounding.

Step 02

In-Line Compounding Control

Continuous melt temperature and pressure monitoring across 10 heating zones in German twin-screw extruders.

Step 03

Sample Test Moulding

ASTM test specimens moulded in-house to verify tensile yield, Izod impact, flexural modulus, and heat deflection.

Step 04

Batch COA Authorization

Formal sign-off by Quality Assurance Head. Sealed pallet labels with QR code linking directly to batch test results.
